The short version

  • OmniBot stores Discord IDs along with the settings, moderation, leveling, and economy data listed below.
  • It never has your Discord password or email.
  • It reads messages live for commands, automod, and leveling, but doesn't store their content.
  • If you use /ask, your prompt is stored with your user ID and the date, and sent to OpenRouter to generate a reply.
  • You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

This summary is for convenience. The full policy below is what applies.

This Privacy Policy explains what data OmniBot ("the Bot") collects, why, and how it's handled. By adding the Bot to a Discord server or using its commands, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data we collect

The Bot stores the following, tied to Discord IDs (server, channel, user, message, and role IDs). The Bot never has access to your Discord password or email.

Server settings
Prefix, channel selections (welcome, leave, boost, and log channels), automod configuration, and command permission settings that you or your server's admins configure.
Moderation records
Warnings, mute, kick, and ban history, and moderator notes created by your server's staff using the Bot. These are tied to the target's Discord user ID and the moderator's Discord user ID.
Leveling data
Your user ID, XP, and level per server, based on message activity.
Economy data
Your user ID and virtual (non-real-money) currency balance per server.
Giveaway participation
Your user ID, if you enter a giveaway.
Custom commands and reaction roles
Text and configuration your server's admins create.
Bot admin and blacklist status
User IDs your server's admins designate as trusted bot admins or block from using the Bot.
AI query logs
If you use the /ask command, your user ID, your prompt text, and the date are stored. Your prompt is also sent to OpenRouter, a third-party AI routing service, to generate a response. See Section 3.

We do not store the full content of your regular messages. Message content is only read in real time to detect commands, check messages against automod filters (banned words, spam, invite and link blocking), and track activity for leveling. None of that scanning is logged or stored beyond the specific records above.

2. Dashboard and login

If you use the OmniBot web dashboard, we use Discord OAuth2 to let you log in. This gives us temporary access to your username, avatar, and the list of servers you're in (specifically, which ones you have Manage Server permission in), solely to show you which servers you can configure.

We do not store your Discord password (Discord never shares it with us) and do not access your Discord messages, friends list, or anything outside what's needed to show you your manageable servers.

3. Third-party services

The Bot relies on these external services, each with its own privacy practices:

MongoDB Atlas
Hosts all the data described above.
OpenRouter
Processes prompts sent through the /ask command to generate AI responses.
Google Translate
Processes text sent through the /translate command. Translated text is not stored by us.
Roblox public API
Used for the /roblox and /robloxstats commands. We only pass along the username you search for, and nothing is stored.
Discord
The platform the Bot operates on. Discord's own Privacy Policy governs your account and messages generally.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the Bot is in your server or until you request deletion. To request deletion of your data (warnings, levels, economy balance, AI query history, and so on), contact us using the details in Section 7.

Removing the Bot from a server does not automatically delete that server's stored data. Request deletion separately if needed.

5. Children's privacy

The Bot is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to use Discord itself (13, or higher where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from users below this age beyond what Discord itself already requires for an account to exist.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Bot's features change. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
